Free offline blackjack simulator.

Supports combinations of the following rules:
1. Number of decks (3–8)
2. Dealer stands on soft 17 / Dealer stands on hard 17.
3. Late surrender, early surrender, or no surrender.
4. DaS – allowed/disallowed.
5. Hole card: European or American rules.
6. Blackjack payout: 3:2 or 6:5.
7. Doubling down: any cards, 10 & 11, or 9, 10 & 11.
